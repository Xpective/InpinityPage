Welcome to Inpinity Staking V2

Two ways to earn passive INPI rewards on Base — classic token staking, or NFT pools that put your Uniswap V4 positions to work.

This quick tour shows you how everything works. Takes about 60 seconds.

💡 You can reopen this tutorial anytime with the "How it works" button.
💧

What is a Liquidity Pool?

A liquidity pool is a shared vault that lets traders swap tokens. When you provide tokens to a pool (e.g. USDC + INPI), you become a Liquidity Provider (LP) and earn a share of trading fees.

  • 💵 You deposit two tokens at equal value
  • 🔄 Traders pay a small fee on every swap
  • 📈 You earn a slice of every trade — automatically

On Uniswap V4, your LP position is represented as an NFT (Token ID).

💰

Why Stake Your LP Position?

Your LP position already earns trading fees. But by staking it here, you unlock 3 more reward streams on top:

  • ⚡ INPI rewards — from a 10-year vesting pool
  • 🚀 Boost multipliers — up to 8× for long locks
  • 🎨 NFT rarity bonus — up to +20% from your Inpinity NFT collection

Your NFT stays 100% yours — the safe only holds it temporarily. On unstake, it always comes back.

🦄

Step 1 — Create your Pool Position

You need a Uniswap V4 LP position before you can stake:

  1. Go to app.uniswap.org/pool
  2. Connect your wallet
  3. Select tokens — e.g. USDC + INPI (contract: 0x232FB1…7d0D)
  4. Choose a fee tier and price range
  5. Enter the amount and confirm in your wallet
  6. You'll receive an NFT (Token ID) representing your position
⚠️ Minimum position value: 10 EUR (or equivalent). For ETH-INPI use native ETH (Uniswap will prompt "Unwrap WETH").
🎯

Step 2 — Stake your NFT

Once you have your Uniswap position NFT:

  1. Find your Token ID on app.uniswap.org/pool → click your position
  2. Come back to this page
  3. Paste the Token ID and select your pool
  4. Choose a lock duration (see next slide)
  5. Click "Approve NFT", then "Stake NFT"

That's it — you're earning! 🎉

🔒
